# The chat card is the lead home

KeyCRM has no API for chats or messages, and a card the site creates cannot receive the messenger thread. The card KeyCRM opens from Telegram, WhatsApp, or Viber is the lead. A click is not a lead: it is kept for 30 days and written onto that card when exactly one unbound click or form on the same channel falls in the 15 minutes before the card. Zero or several candidates stay untouched, and a manager can paste the lead id later. A messenger form with no chat after 15 minutes still becomes its own `Лід N`. Empty click cards were rejected because they filled the pipeline with people who never wrote.
